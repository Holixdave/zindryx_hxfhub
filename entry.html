Made for students who prepare alone

Zindryx started as one developer’s project and grew with what students needed.

Why “Zindryx”?
It was a word nobody had used, so it became ours. We tied it to a meaning: Genesis, a beginning.
  1. The sparkAnswering past questions alone is hard, and students who prepare by themselves can feel left behind.
  2. A solo buildOne developer built the first version, shaped by what students actually needed.
  3. Practise togetherThe answer was a synced exam room: many students, the same questions, in real time.
  4. A growing teamA small team now builds Zindryx together, with privacy and safety built in.
